Fish Collection

You gain the Fishing Rod and the ability to fish on El Dorado's Ship. However, there's little reason to actually fish (other than for fun) until you complete the quest Bee Problems. After completing this quest, you can find the Fish Collector, Dougal, in Kristina's Room in Noble's House.

As with Food and Flowers, not all Fish that can be found at a fishing spot will be available every time you enter a location. In my testing, there are approximately five types of Fish that can be caught at each location per entry, with some fishing spots having a few more and some having a few less. This means that in order to complete the collection, you may have to exit and re-enter a location multiple times depending on your luck. Additionally, the Marlin can only be caught after completing the quest The Marlin Bait, which can be accidentally missed if you're not careful.

Fish Book

Fish Collector Rewards

There are four categories of Fish: Small fresh water fish, Large fresh water fish, Small sea fish, and Large sea fish. Giving Dougal all the fish in a category awards you with an Anc Water, the most powerful healing item in the game, giving you up to four Anc Waters from fishing. Additionally, completing the entire Fish Book rewards you with the Fish Master Hat for your dog to wear.

White Fish

White Fish can be found in the pond near the large tree in Green Meadows.
