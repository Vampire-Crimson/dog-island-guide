Bug Collection

You gain the Bug Cage and the ability to catch Bugs during the quest Challenge with Clark! After getting the Bug Cage, the Bug Collector Derrick shows up in Kristina's Room.

Unlike the other collection categories, Bugs can be somewhat complicated to find. Most Bugs only appear at certain times of day, and the timing can be tight. Another issue is that, unlike other collectables, the individual locations are not randomly generated. Instead, when you enter an area, the game chooses from premade patterns. While this can make blind Bug-hunting difficult and frustrating, identifying these patterns can remove the "hunting" aspect entirely. For example, you can go into and out of an area during the day until you find the pattern you need for a Bug that only appears at twilight, then just go to the location where that Bug will appear and wait for the sun to set. This guide is written with strategy included, so that you don't need to learn the patterns themselves and can instead focus on simply catching the Bugs at their most convenient locations and completing the collection.

Bug Book

Bug Collector Rewards

There are four categories of Bugs: Bugs with beautiful wings, Bugs that fly straight, Hard shelled bugs that live on trees, and Bugs that live on grass and flowers. Giving Derrick all the Bugs in one category will reward you with an Anc Water, the best healing item in the game, giving you up to four Anc Waters from Bug hunting. Additionally, completing the entire Bug Book rewards you with the Bag Master Hat (a misspelling of "Bug Master Hat") for your dog to wear.

Cabbage Butterfly

The Cabbage Butterfly can be found in Green Meadows, Treely Woods, Zoomy Lake, Hoya Plains, Gola Highlands, and the Ancient Grove. In most locations, it only appears during the day, or rarely in the early morning. However, in Zoomy Lake it is guaranteed to appear in a single spot at almost all times of day (with only a 1/4 chance to not appear at night). Simply go to the beach near the exit to Treely Woods, bark at the snake, and catch the butterfly.
